# BetterHealth Social Copy Rules

## Voice

Use warm, plain, patient-facing language.

Lead with the clearest health idea, then make the next step concrete.

Prefer:
- "Your blood can give useful clues."
- "A Full Blood Count is a starting point, not the whole story."
- "Don't panic. Seek clarity."
- "Use the result to make a better health decision."
- "One result outside the reference range needs context before conclusions."
- "BetterHealth helps you choose the right tests, get tested, and understand your results in plain English."

Avoid:
- jargon before plain English;
- fear-heavy wording;
- claiming a test diagnoses the exact cause;
- sounding like the brand knows everything;
- vague abstract process language.

## Forbidden Lines

Do not use these exact lines:
- "The goal is not to panic. The goal is to get a clearer next step."
- "Symptoms are signals. They are not a diagnosis."

## Health Claims

Use careful phrases:
- "can help"
- "may help"
- "useful clues"
- "often a useful place to begin"
- "needs context"
- "read with symptoms, history, medicines, and other tests where needed"

Do not say:
- "will reveal"
- "proves"
- "diagnoses"
- "always the right place to start"
- "rules out everything"

## Footer Disclaimer

Use on health education assets:

`General education only. For severe or urgent symptoms, seek medical care.`
