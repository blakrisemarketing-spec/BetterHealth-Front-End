# BetterHealth Image Prompt Patterns

Generate backgrounds without text. Add copy in HTML/CSS.

## Photorealistic Medical Explainer Background

Use this structure:

```text
Use case: scientific-educational
Asset type: Instagram social visual background, <dimensions>
Primary request: create a realistic, immersive medical macro scene focused on <concept>.
Scene/backdrop: <bloodstream / organ / test-domain scene>.
Subject: <main biological subject> is the clear focus.
Style/medium: photorealistic macro medical visualization, realistic textures, cinematic depth of field, high detail.
Composition/framing: leave a readable lighter cream/soft haze area for text overlay; keep the richest subject detail on the opposite side.
Lighting/mood: warm, trustworthy, calm, clinically credible; not alarming.
Color palette: warm cream, translucent beige, deep natural reds where medically relevant, muted sage accents.
Text: none.
Constraints: no words, no labels, no logo, no watermark, no test tubes, no syringes, no flat vector illustration, no cartoon cells, no scary disease imagery.
```

## Blood Component Concepts

Red blood cells:
- Focus on oxygen delivery.
- Show realistic biconcave red cells flowing through a vessel.
- Optional subtle golden oxygen-like highlights; avoid sci-fi excess.

White blood cells:
- Focus on immune response.
- Show one textured white blood cell as hero, with red cells in context.

Platelets:
- Focus on clotting support.
- Show small platelet fragments/clusters near a subtle vessel-wall repair area.
- Avoid gore, wounds, blood splatter, or alarm.

Full Blood Count limits:
- Show blood/testing context plus translucent clinical forms for other domains: glucose, liver, kidney, thyroid, lipid/cholesterol.
- Avoid flat organ icons.

Next steps / clarity:
- Show blood cells moving through a softly illuminated path with translucent result panels.
- Mood should feel reassuring and resolved.

## Ghana-Market Human Story Prompt

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: BetterHealth social lifestyle visual, <dimensions>
Primary request: <human action>.
Scene/backdrop: ordinary Ghanaian home/room/clinic context, lived-in and realistic, not luxury-stock.
Subject: real/photorealistic Ghanaian person, natural posture and expression.
Composition/framing: overhead or over-the-shoulder if a phone screen must be visible; keep the person and relevant object centered.
Lighting/mood: warm natural light, candid, trustworthy, real.
Constraints: no illustrated humans, no cartoon, no abstract person, no luxury interior, no watermark, no in-image text unless explicitly needed.
```
