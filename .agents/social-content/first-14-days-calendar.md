## Launch Sprint Goals

- Establish the core BetterHealth idea.
- Learn which health concerns produce saves, comments, DMs, and clicks.
- Build early trust without fearmongering.
- Start a repeatable series library.

## Daily Publishing Cadence

- Instagram: 1 feed post or Reel, plus 2-4 Stories.
- Facebook: repost/adapt the Instagram caption with a slightly warmer community tone.
- LinkedIn: 3 posts per week, focused on preventive health, workplace health, trust, and health-system clarity.
- X: optional daily short post or thread repurposed from the main idea.
