# BetterHealth Social Content Pipeline

Start date: 2026-07-14
Program length: 100 days

This pipeline turns the Copywriting Council into a weekly social publishing system for BetterHealth.

## Operating Principle

Do not create random daily posts. Create repeatable content series that make BetterHealth known for one idea:

> BetterHealth helps people move from health uncertainty to confidence through the right tests, simple access, and easy to understand results.

## Weekly Pipeline

### Monday: Strategy and Briefs

- Pick one weekly theme.
- Choose 5-7 post topics.
- Assign each post a primary job: awareness, education, trust, conversion, or engagement.
- Route the weekly batch through the relevant council lenses.

Recommended council sequence:

1. `Copywriting Council Chair`: choose the agents and define the brief.
2. `Breakthrough Advertising`: awareness stage, desire, mechanism, proof gap.
3. `Positioning`: category, contrast, mental slot.
4. `Ogilvy on Advertising`: trust, proof, main selling idea.
5. `Scientific Advertising`: testable offer and CTA.
6. `Tested Advertising Methods`: hooks.
7. `The Copywriter's Handbook`: practical structure and clarity.
8. `Influence`: ethical persuasion and trust.
9. `Made to Stick`: memorability.
10. `Copywriting Council Chair`: final synthesis.

### Tuesday: Draft

- Write one core post per day.
- Adapt each post into:
  - Instagram carousel or Reel
  - LinkedIn post
  - Facebook post
  - X thread or short post
  - Story prompt

### Wednesday: Trust and Clinical Review

Check every post for:

- Unsupported health claims
- Fear-based language
- Medical diagnosis language
- Overpromising
- Missing next step
- Unclear test/process explanation

### Thursday: Design and Scheduling

- Create carousel graphics and short-form scripts.
- Schedule 5-7 posts.
- Prepare story frames and engagement prompts.

### Friday: Learning Review

Track:

- Saves
- Shares
- Comments
- DMs
- Profile visits
- Link clicks
- Book-test clicks
- Follower growth

### Daily Engagement

Spend 20-30 minutes:

- Reply to all comments.
- Turn common questions into new post ideas.
- Comment thoughtfully on 5-10 Ghana health, wellness, professional, parenting, employer, and clinician posts.
- Log objections and exact audience language.

## Content Rules

- Do not revive the retired fallback formulations about panic and clearer next steps, or about symptoms and diagnosis.
- Preferred phrasing: "Symptoms are signals. It's your body's way of telling you something is not right."
- Preferred reassurance: "Don't panic. Seek clarity."
- Preferred outcome: "move from worry to confidence" or "make informed decisions about your health."
- Say "right tests", "better information", "easy to understand results", and "clear next steps" rather than promising diagnosis or cure.
- Use "can help" and "may indicate" for educational health content.
- Do not tell someone they have a condition based on symptoms.
- Encourage urgent care for severe or emergency symptoms.
- Use simple language before medical terminology.
- Prefer examples, process, and proof over hype.
- Keep CTAs practical: book a test, learn what to check, DM a question, save the post.
