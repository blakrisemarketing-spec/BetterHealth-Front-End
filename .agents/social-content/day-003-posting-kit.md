Pillar: Health Worry to Clear Next Step

## Council Review Synthesis

This post should meet people where they are: tired, busy, and half-convinced they should "just rest more."

Council diagnosis:
- Breakthrough Advertising: the audience is problem-aware. They know the tiredness is real, but they do not know whether it is sleep, stress, blood, sugar, thyroid, iron, or something else. Lead with that uncertainty.
- Tested Advertising Methods: use a direct symptom hook, then give a short list of possible checks. Specificity makes the content save-worthy.
- The Copywriter's Handbook: avoid a long medical explanation. Give the practical "what to ask about" list.
- Ogilvy on Advertising: preserve trust by saying tests help narrow the question, not "find the answer" automatically.
- Influence: reduce friction with a simple action: DM "TIRED" or book a health test.
- Made to Stick: make the repeatable line concrete: "Tired is a symptom, not a plan."
- Chair synthesis: make this a next-step post, not a diagnosis post. The message is: if tiredness keeps showing up, check what your body may be trying to tell you.

## Core Message

Feeling tired all the time can come from many places: sleep, stress, medicines, mood, infections, anaemia, iron levels, thyroid changes, diabetes, liver or kidney problems, and other health conditions. A health test will not explain every cause of tiredness by itself, but the right checks can give useful clues and help you make a better next decision.

## Clinical Guardrail

Do not say a test will identify the exact cause of tiredness. Do not make fatigue sound like it is usually one thing. Use "can help", "may help", "worth discussing", and "needs context".

If tiredness is severe, sudden, worsening, linked with chest pain, shortness of breath, fainting, confusion, severe weakness, unexplained weight loss, heavy bleeding, or other worrying symptoms, seek medical care urgently.

Do not reuse the two retired Day 1 guardrail lines already documented in the pipeline rules.

Preferred framing:
- "Tired is a symptom, not a plan."
- "Don't just push through it. Check what may be going on."
- "A test can help you stop guessing and ask a better question."
- "The right check depends on your symptoms, history, medicines, and risk factors."
- "Don't panic. Seek clarity."

## LinkedIn Post

"I am always tired" is one of those health concerns many people normalize.

They tell themselves:

It is work.
It is stress.
It is poor sleep.
It is life.

Sometimes that is true.

But persistent tiredness can also be linked to health questions worth investigating: anaemia, iron levels, thyroid changes, diabetes, liver or kidney problems, medicines, sleep disorders, infections, mood, and more.

That does not mean one test explains everything.

It means the right test can help make the next conversation more useful.

Depending on the person, checks worth discussing may include:

1. Full Blood Count
For blood-related clues such as anaemia or infection patterns.

2. Thyroid Function
For thyroid-related changes that may affect energy.

3. Blood Sugar / HbA1c
For diabetes-related clues.

4. Iron, B12, or Vitamin D
When deficiency is part of the question.

5. Liver and Kidney Function
When symptoms, medicines, or history point in that direction.

The key is context: symptoms, health history, medicines, lifestyle, and any other tests a clinician recommends.

## Design Notes

- Primary format is a Reel, but create a strong static cover frame.
- Cover frame should be Strict Brand Image or Real-Life Human Story, not an immersive medical explainer.
- If using a human image, use a realistic Ghanaian bedroom or work-desk scene with lived-in details.
- If using brand-only design, use warm cream background, BetterHealth logo, large headline, and clean checklist card.
- Avoid making the person look dangerously ill or helpless.
- Keep "Tired all the time?" as the first visible hook.
- Keep the DM "TIRED" CTA large enough to read on mobile.
- Do not use flat illustrated people.

## Success Metrics

- Reel watch-through rate

## Hypothesis

The familiar symptom hook will create more comments and DMs than a test-led post because the audience already recognizes the problem. The list of checks gives them a concrete next step without pretending tiredness has one simple cause.
