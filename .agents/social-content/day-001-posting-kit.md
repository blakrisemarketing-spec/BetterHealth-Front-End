## Core Message

Your body is always talking. BetterHealth helps you listen. When something feels off, the right test can help you seek better information and make informed decisions about your health.

## Clinical Guardrail

Do not imply that a test alone diagnoses every condition or replaces clinical care. Use "can help", "may show", and "next step". For severe, sudden, or worsening symptoms, advise urgent medical care.

Do not revive the retired fallback formulations about panic and clearer next steps, or about symptoms and diagnosis.

Preferred framing:
- "Symptoms are signals. It's your body's way of telling you something is not right."
- "Don't panic. Seek clarity."
- "The right next step is not to panic or self-medicate. Rather, seek better information through testing."
- "Move from worry to confidence."
