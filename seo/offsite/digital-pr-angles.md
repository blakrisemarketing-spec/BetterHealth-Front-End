# Digital PR & Backlink Angles

Earned links from credible sites move competitive rankings and strengthen E-E-A-T
(important for health/YMYL content). Each angle below is a story a journalist,
blogger, or partner can run with — pair it with data and a named spokesperson.

## Angles

1. **Founder story.** CEO's diabetes diagnosis (UK, 2022, DKA) and reversal in 18
   months → mission to make screening affordable in Ghana. Strong human-interest +
   health hook. Target: Ghanaian tech/health/business press, founder-interview
   podcasts and newsletters.

2. **Health-equity data.** "Comprehensive screening costs GHS 2,970–6,250 at private
   hospitals — out of reach for most Ghanaians." Frame BetterHealth as closing the
   gap. Target: development/health-policy outlets, op-eds.

3. **100 Healthy Years Foundation.** Free community screening events → local-news
   coverage and partner link pages (NGOs, faith orgs, employers, universities).
   Each event is a citation + backlink opportunity.

4. **Silent-disease awareness.** "1 in 3 Ghanaian adults has hypertension and many
   don't know it." Tie to awareness days (World Diabetes Day, World Hypertension Day,
   World Kidney Day) for timely pitches and linkable data pages.

5. **Free LIMS for labs.** B2B angle — "free, forever lab software" → health-tech and
   lab/industry press, partner co-marketing.

## Linkable assets to build (feed the content roadmap)

- A "State of preventive health screening in Ghana" data page (original stats,
  highly citable by press and AI engines).
- Biomarker reference-range explainers (already in the blog roadmap) — evergreen,
  attract natural links.

## Execution notes

- Always pitch with a named spokesperson and a real quote.
- Offer an exclusive data point or first look to higher-authority targets.
