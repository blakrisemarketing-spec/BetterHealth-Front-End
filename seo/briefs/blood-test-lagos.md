## Citable one-sentence claims (GEO-ready, answer-first for each H2)

- Most people in Lagos get a blood test for one of five reasons: a routine checkup, working out a specific symptom, a pre-employment or insurance requirement, antenatal care, or premarital genotype screening.
- In Lagos, a blood test can be done at a general hospital laboratory, a standalone diagnostic lab, or through a home-sample-collection service that sends a phlebotomist to you.
- Lagos traffic can turn a routine blood test into a half-day commitment once travel, waiting, and the return trip are counted, which is why home sample collection has become a genuine local option rather than a niche convenience.
- A standard full checkup panel in Lagos commonly includes a full blood count, fasting blood sugar, a lipid profile, genotype, blood group, hepatitis B, and HIV screening.
- Lagos's disease pattern makes hypertension, diabetes, typhoid, malaria, and sickle cell/genotype status the conditions most worth testing for, alongside whatever a doctor recommends for your specific symptoms.
- Choosing where to test in Lagos comes down to three things: is the lab accredited, is your result handled privately, and will you get a clear answer on when to expect your result.
