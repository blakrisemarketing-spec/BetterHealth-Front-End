## Angle
Plain-language explainer covering the fasting blood sugar test: what it is, what
normal looks like, what prediabetes/diabetes thresholds are, and what to do with
the result. Include a ranges chart block and cover "what level is dangerous" to
capture the 590/mo secondary. Ghana framing: mmol/L is standard in Ghanaian labs.

## H2 outline (each opens with a self-contained citable claim)

1. **What "fasting" means** — You need to have eaten nothing for at least 8 hours
   before the test; water is fine.
2. **Fasting blood sugar normal ranges** — WHO/ADA thresholds in mmol/L and mg/dL;
   include a visual ranges block.
3. **What a high fasting result means** — prediabetes (5.6–6.9 mmol/L) and diabetes
   (≥ 7.0 mmol/L), confirmed on second test.
4. **What level of blood sugar is dangerous** — hypoglycaemia (< 3.9 mmol/L) and
   hyperglycaemic crisis (> 16 mmol/L). Covers the 590/mo secondary.
5. **Fasting vs random blood sugar test** — when each is used; covers
   "random blood sugar normal range" secondary.
6. **How it compares to HbA1c** — snapshot vs average; when to use each.
7. **What to do with your result** — next steps, internal link graph.
