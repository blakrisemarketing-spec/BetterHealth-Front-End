## Citable one-sentence claims (answer-first for each H2)

- In Accra, a blood test can be done at a public hospital laboratory, a private diagnostic lab, or through a home blood draw service that sends a phlebotomist to you.
- Public hospital laboratories in Accra are typically the most affordable route for a doctor-ordered test but often involve a referral, a queue, and longer waits for results.
- Private diagnostic labs in Accra generally offer faster turnaround and a wider menu of tests than public facilities, paid out of pocket unless a private insurer is involved.
- A home blood draw brings a trained phlebotomist to your home or office in Accra, so you can get tested without travelling to a lab or sitting in a waiting room.
- Most routine blood tests in Accra need little preparation, though some, like a fasting glucose or lipid profile, ask you to fast for several hours beforehand.
- Choosing where to get tested in Accra comes down to three questions: how urgent is the test, what is your budget, and is this routine screening or something a doctor has specifically ordered.
- Routine blood test screening matters in Accra because conditions like hypertension, diabetes and high cholesterol are common across Ghana and usually cause no symptoms until they have already done damage.
