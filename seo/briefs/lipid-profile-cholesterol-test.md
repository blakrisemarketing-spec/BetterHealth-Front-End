## Angle

mean, how to read the normal ranges, and why fasting is required. Cover LDL vs
HDL to capture that secondary. Ghana framing: mmol/L is standard in Ghanaian labs;
palm oil-heavy diet and rising CVD burden are relevant context.

## H2 outline (each opens with a self-contained citable claim)

1. **What a lipid profile test measures** — A lipid profile is a blood test that
   measures four fats: total cholesterol, LDL, HDL, and triglycerides.
2. **Lipid profile normal ranges** — ACC/AHA 2018 thresholds for all four; mmol/L
   and mg/dL side by side.
3. **LDL vs HDL: what "bad" and "good" actually mean** — LDL deposits cholesterol
   in artery walls; HDL removes it. High LDL + low HDL is the pattern that drives
   cardiovascular risk.
4. **Why the test requires fasting** — Triglycerides rise significantly after a meal;
   9 to 12 hours of fasting is needed for an accurate result.
5. **What a high or low result means** — No single number is a verdict on its own;
   the pattern (LDL + HDL + triglycerides together) is what matters.
6. **What to do with your results** — next steps, internal link graph.
