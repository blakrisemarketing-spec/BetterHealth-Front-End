## Angle
This is an answer-first HUB, not a third deep-dive on glucose ranges. It exists to compare
the four diabetes test types side by side (fasting glucose, random glucose, HbA1c, OGTT),
explain when each is ordered, and answer the home-testing question honestly, then send the
reader on to the dedicated hba1c-explained and fasting-blood-sugar-explained articles for
full range detail rather than repeating it. Ranges cited here are restated consistently
with those two articles and with prediabetes-warning-signs.js, not re-derived.

## H2 outline (each opens with a self-contained citable claim)

1. **Types overview** — There are four main diabetes tests: fasting plasma glucose, random
   blood glucose, HbA1c, and the OGTT, each measuring blood sugar a different way. Covers
   "types of blood sugar test" and "diabetes test name" secondaries.
2. **Fasting glucose** — Measures glucose after an 8-hour fast; diabetes cutoff ≥ 7.0 mmol/L
   on two tests. Links out to fasting-blood-sugar-explained for the full range breakdown.
3. **Random glucose** — Measures glucose at any time; used with symptoms for a fast diagnosis;
   cutoff ≥ 11.1 mmol/L plus symptoms.
4. **HbA1c** — Measures a 2-3 month average, no fasting needed; diabetes cutoff ≥ 6.5%. Links
   out to hba1c-explained for the full range breakdown and the sickle cell trait caveat.
5. **OGTT** — A challenge test: fasting baseline + 75g glucose drink + 2-hour reading; diabetes
   cutoff ≥ 11.1 mmol/L at 2 hours. Ordered in pregnancy (24-28 weeks) and to confirm borderline
   results.
6. **Which test, when** — Decision-oriented comparison list: routine screening vs symptoms vs
   pregnancy vs borderline result vs already-diagnosed monitoring.
7. **Home testing** — A home glucometer is for day-to-day monitoring once diagnosed, not a
   diagnostic substitute for a lab test. Directly and honestly answers "how to test for
   diabetes at home" without suggesting home testing replaces a diagnosis.
8. **What to do** — Practical next-step list by scenario.
