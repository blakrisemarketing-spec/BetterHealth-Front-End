## Citable claim per H2
- Premarital screening has become a routine, expected step before marriage across much of Nigeria and West Africa, driven mainly by the high regional carrier rate for the sickle cell gene.
- A standard premarital panel covers haemoglobin genotype, ABO and Rh blood group, HIV, hepatitis B and C, and syphilis, usually drawn from a single blood sample.
- Genotype and blood group results matter most for family planning, since they flag sickle cell risk for children and Rh incompatibility risk during pregnancy, not disqualifying facts about either partner.
- Infectious disease screening protects both partners and any future children, and a positive result is a starting point for treatment or precautions, not a reason to skip the wedding.
- A concerning result, whether genotype, Rh, or an infectious disease marker, is a prompt for genetic or medical counselling before conception, not an emergency to react to alone.
