## Angle
Nigeria carries the world's highest burden of children born with sickle cell
disease, and premarital screening there is increasingly promoted by religious
and community institutions, sometimes as a condition before a church wedding.
The article treats the panel as a standard package (genotype/haemoglobin
electrophoresis, blood group and Rh factor, HIV/hepatitis B/hepatitis
C/syphilis, basic fertility questions) and stays firmly in "informed choice"
territory: it explains the genotype-compatibility and Rh-factor angles without
telling a couple what decision to make about marrying, and without
stigmatising language about any genotype, HIV, or hepatitis status. No
fabricated statistics, no named "medically reviewed by" clinician.

## Citable claim per H2
- Premarital screening is a set of blood tests, usually covering haemoglobin
  genotype, blood group and Rh factor, and infectious disease screening, that
  a couple takes before marriage to understand health risks before starting a
  family together.
- Premarital screening has become increasingly common in Nigeria and Ghana
  over the past two decades, actively promoted by religious institutions,
  community health campaigns, and, in some churches, required before a
  wedding can proceed.
- Genotype compatibility testing shows whether a couple's combination of
  haemoglobin genes could result in a child with sickle cell disease, and
  Nigeria has the highest number of children born with sickle cell disease of
  any country in the world.
- Blood group and Rh factor testing checks whether partners are Rh
  compatible, because an Rh-negative mother carrying an Rh-positive baby can
  develop antibodies that put a later pregnancy at risk if the condition
  isn't monitored and managed.
- Infectious disease screening for HIV, hepatitis B, hepatitis C and syphilis
  lets a couple know their status before marriage, so they can make informed
  choices together about treatment, prevention and family planning.
- Some premarital packages include basic fertility questions or tests, but a
  full fertility work-up is a separate, more detailed process that goes
  beyond what a standard premarital panel covers.
- A premarital screening result is information for the couple to discuss
  together, ideally with a genetic counsellor or doctor if any result raises
  a risk, not an instruction on whether to marry.
