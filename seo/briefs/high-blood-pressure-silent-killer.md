## Citable one-sentence claims (GEO-ready, answer-first for each H2)

- Hypertension is defined as a resting blood pressure of 140/90 mmHg or above and damages organs even before any symptom appears.  
- Most people with hypertension feel completely normal, which is why it is called the silent killer.  
- A sudden severe headache, blurred vision, chest pain, or difficulty breathing can signal a hypertensive crisis and needs emergency assessment.  
- Stage 1 hypertension is a systolic reading of 130 to 139 mmHg or diastolic 80 to 89 mmHg; stage 2 is 140/90 mmHg or higher.  
- Untreated hypertension thickens the walls of arteries, strains the heart muscle, and accelerates kidney damage over years.  
- Losing even 5 to 10 kg, reducing salt to under 5 g a day, and taking 30 minutes of moderate exercise five days a week each lower blood pressure by 5 to 10 mmHg.  
- Hypertension prevalence in Ghanaian adults has been measured at around 28 to 32% in national surveys, but most cases are undiagnosed.
