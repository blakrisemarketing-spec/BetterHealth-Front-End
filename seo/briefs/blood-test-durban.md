## Angle

Second South-Africa-market article on the site, a sibling to blood-test-johannesburg.js
(drafted in parallel). Must read as genuinely Durban/KwaZulu-Natal specific, not a
city-name swap of the Johannesburg piece. The differentiating angle:

1. Durban is framed explicitly as South Africa's busiest port city and a coastal
   metro with its own geographic spread (city centre, Umhlanga, Pinetown,
   Westville), which is the real, non-hard-sell reason home sample collection is
   worth mentioning as a category (travel time, not a sales pitch).
2. South Africa's private-pay model (medical aid schemes, gap cover, co-payments)
   is described in general, hedged terms only, no coverage amounts stated. NHI
   (National Health Insurance) reform is mentioned as an ongoing, evolving process,
   never as already fully implemented.
3. Inkosi Albert Luthuli Central Hospital named once as the widely known public
   tertiary hospital example for KwaZulu-Natal, used illustratively, not as a
   specific-services claim.
4. KwaZulu-Natal's historically high HIV burden is named honestly and respectfully
   (qualitative "one of South Africa's highest" framing, no invented precise
   statistic), alongside a rising burden of hypertension and diabetes, consistent
   with the disease-burden framing already used in hiv-test-explained.js and
   malaria-test-explained.js on this site.
5. Standard three-route framework (public hospital, private pathology lab, home
   sample collection) carried over from the Accra/Lagos/Nairobi/Kumasi/Johannesburg
   local pages, retargeted with Durban-specific facility types (private pathology
   groups, not generic "private lab") and geography.
