# Wellness Plan — Template

**For:** BetterHealth Wellness Consultants
**Delivered:** WhatsApp (PDF), within 24 hours of the consultation
**Length:** 2 pages. Not 3. A plan someone won't read is not a plan.

---

## Why this document exists

The plan **is** the product. Everything before it — the ad, the landing page, the
call — exists to produce this. If someone books no test at all but keeps this on
their phone and shows it to their spouse, the campaign has still worked, because
that is the artefact that brings them back.

Three rules that decide whether it lands:

1. **Use their words.** If they said "I get tired by 2pm and I don't know why",
   the plan says that. Not "fatigue". Their sentence, in quotes, on page one.
2. **At least one action must be free and startable today.** A plan where every
   step costs money is a quotation wearing a plan's clothes.
3. **It must survive being read by their doctor.** No diagnosis, no interpretation,
   no medicine advice. Recommendations and questions only.

### Wellness Plan for Kwame Mensah

**Prepared by** Akosua, Wellness Consultant, BetterHealth
**Date** 12 August 2026 · **Reference** WC-4471

---

#### What you told me

> "My father had a stroke at 58. I'm 41 and I've never checked anything."

You feel well day to day, but you've noticed you're heavier than you were two years
ago and you're tired in the afternoons in a way you didn't used to be. You've never
had blood work done. What sits at the back of your mind is your father, and whether
you're on the same road he was.

---

#### Where you are now

Right now the honest answer is that we don't know — and neither does anyone else,
because none of it has ever been checked. That's the starting point, not a problem.
The good news is you're asking the question at 41 rather than at 58.

**What I'd check first**

| What | Why, in plain English | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **Cardion — Heart Health Check** | Your cholesterol and the markers tied to blood pressure and heart risk. These are the ones that matter most given your father's stroke, and they can give useful clues years before anyone feels anything. | **GHS 475** |

Results come back in 48 to 72 hours. A doctor then goes through them with you, and
we update this plan based on what turns up.

*If you'd rather start smaller, a Lipid/Cholesterol Panel on its own is GHS 120 and
answers the single biggest question here. We can add the rest later.*

---

#### Your three goals

**1. Know my numbers, so I stop guessing about my heart.**
One round of blood work, then a doctor's read on it. After that this stops being a
worry and starts being a number you can watch.

**2. Walk 30 minutes, four days a week.**
You said evenings after work are realistic. Start at three days if four feels like
a stretch — the streak matters more than the target.

**3. Cut the afternoon soft drink.**
You mentioned one most days. Swap it for water and we'll see whether the 2pm slump
shifts on its own.

---

#### Your first 30 days

| When | What |
|---|---|
| **This week** | Start the evening walks — three days is a win. Drop the afternoon soft drink. Neither costs anything. |
| **Within 2 weeks** | Book the Cardion check. Home collection is available in Accra if getting to a lab is the hard part. |
| **Week 3** | Doctor consultation to go through your results and update this plan. |
| **Week 4** | Check in with me — has the afternoon tiredness changed? |

---

#### Let's check back on 10 November 2026

Three months gives the walking and the drink change enough time to show up in your
cholesterol numbers. If we've got a baseline from this month, we'll have something
real to compare against rather than starting over.

## Quality checklist — before you send

- [ ] Their own words are quoted on page one
- [ ] At least one action is free and can start this week
- [ ] The panel recommendation is tied to something they actually said
- [ ] Nothing in the document diagnoses, interprets a result, or mentions medicines
- [ ] A cheaper honest starting option is offered if cost came up on the call
- [ ] Goals are things a person could do on a Tuesday
- [ ] Reference code appears twice — header and footer
- [ ] Two pages or fewer
- [ ] Disclaimer present
- [ ] Sent within 24 hours of the call

## Production note

Build this as a Google Docs template with the BetterHealth wordmark, Quicksand
throughout, and the teal used sparingly for headings. Consultants duplicate, fill,
export to PDF, send on WhatsApp. Filename: `Wellness-Plan-[FirstName]-[REF].pdf`.

Do not automate the writing of these for v1. The whole reason the document works is
that it is visibly written by a person who listened.
