## Compliance pass (Meta, health, personal attributes)

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Personal attributes | Pass. No "you have", no condition questions, no age or body callouts aimed at the reader. Gender-targeted sets (Alpha, Empress) describe the product ("for men from 40"), not the reader. |
| Sensational or negative language | Pass. Removed: killer, death, silent killer, damage, before illness strikes. |
| Health claims | Pass. "checks", "shows where things stand", "can pick up early signs", "useful for spotting". No "diagnoses", "reveals", "prevents". |
| Disease names | Group statements with a cited source (IDF, Ghana Health Service) or as what a test measures. |
| Before/after | None. |
| Open loops and clickbait | Pass. Every ad names what is on the page. |
| Landing page elements | Guides carry the disclaimer, privacy link, exit nav, no auto-play, no timers. Panel pages already comply. |
| Ad-to-page consistency | Headlines match page h1s (panel display names; guide titles verbatim). Same domain, no redirect chains (trailing-slash URLs avoid the LiteSpeed 301). |

Score: 10/10 on the checklist. Residual risk is reviewer variance on the diabetes and blood-pressure ad sets; if any is rejected, the fallback is to drop the group statistic sentence and run Ad A alone.
